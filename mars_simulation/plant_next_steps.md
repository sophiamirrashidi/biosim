# Plant Model & Cyber-Physical System: Architecture, Status, and Next Steps

**Status as of November 2026.**  
The bioregenerative life support simulator combines NASA BioSim (Java physical plant) with a first-class Python Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) layer in `mars_simulation/`. This document outlines the decided architecture, the surgical BioSim patches applied, implemented subsystems, current fault detection status, and active next steps for the team.

---

## 1. Decided Architecture & Conventions

- **Hybrid Plant Architecture:**
  - **BioSim (Java):** Acts as the underlying physical plant modeling crop biochemistry (lettuce canopy closure, biomass accumulation, transpiration), water store mass balances, gas store kinetics, and the basic electrical network.
  - **Python CPS Layer (`mars_simulation/`):** First-class owner of exterior boundary conditions (Martian sol diurnal temperature cycle), continuous thermal energy balance ODEs, sensor physics (lag, noise, calibration drift, optical cross-sensitivity), state estimators/monitors, closed-loop cyber controllers, and supervisory arbitration.
- **Strict Separation of Ground Truth vs Observations:**
  - `truth` generated by `plant.step()` represents true physical state.
  - Hidden variables and ground-truth values (`gh.temp_truth_C`, `crop.s0.reservoir_L`, `faults.active`, `sim.ended`) are registered in `TRUTH_ONLY_KEYS` in [`schema.py`](interface/schema.py).
  - The tick loop ([`loop.py`](interface/loop.py)) strips truth-only keys before passing `obs` to the sensor layer and controllers. Sensor layers access hidden state exclusively via `ctx.truth` to simulate physical hardware sensor taps.
- **Monitor-in-the-Loop Pattern ([`estimators/thermal.py`](estimators/thermal.py)):**
  - To prevent sensor faults (e.g., negative bias) from corrupting closed-loop control, `MonitorInLoop` splits monitoring across two pipeline hooks:
    1. **Sensor Layer Hook:** Runs estimators against raw telemetry, isolates sensor faults, computes fused state (`fused_temp_C`), and injects detection flags into `obs`.
    2. **Supervisor Hook:** Steps the internal dynamic models using the final arbitrated commands.
  - Controllers regulate on fused estimates rather than unverified sensor readings.
- **Dual Backend Support:**
  - [`BioSimPlant`](interface/plant.py): Connects over REST to the live BioSim container on `localhost:8009`.
  - [`FakePlant`](interface/plant.py): Fast, deterministic standalone Python model mirroring BioSim's state keys and dynamics for 100% offline development and CI.

## 2. BioSim Java Patches & Surgical Coupling

Upstream BioSim had several historical bugs and hard-coded values that were surgically patched:

1. **Mutable Environment Temperature & REST Injection:**
   - Upstream `SimEnvironment.java` hard-coded `private final static float temperature = 23f`.
   - Converted `temperature` into a mutable instance field in [`SimEnvironment.java`](../src/com/traclabs/biosim/server/simulation/environment/SimEnvironment.java) and added `POST /api/simulation/{simID}/modules/{moduleName}/temperature` in [`SimulationController.java`](../src/com/traclabs/biosim/server/framework/SimulationController.java).
2. **Thermal-Biological Crop Coupling ($\eta_T$):**
   - Implemented `calculateThermalEfficiency(float tempC)` in [`Plant.java`](../src/com/traclabs/biosim/server/simulation/food/Plant.java).
   - Computes a dimensionless efficiency factor $\eta_T(T) \in [0, 1]$ centered at optimal $22^\circ\text{C}$, dropping smoothly to $0$ at freezing ($0^\circ\text{C}$) and heat limits ($35^\circ\text{C}$), scaling `dailyCarbonGain *= myLastThermalEfficiency`.
3. **Transpiration Bug Fix (`MathUtils.exp`):**
   - In `src/com/traclabs/biosim/server/util/MathUtils.java`, `exp(double a)` mistakenly returned `Math.abs(a)` instead of $e^a$ (an upstream bug since 2008).
   - The plant calculated saturated vapor pressure as 0.93 kPa instead of 2.81 kPa at 23 °C, clamping vapor pressure deficit to 0. **No crop in BioSim ever transpired.**
   - Fixed `MathUtils.exp(a)` to call `Math.exp(a)`. Crops now transpire naturally based on vapor pressure deficit and stomatal conductance.
4. **Harmonized Relative Humidity Saturation Curve:**
   - Harmonized `SimEnvironment.saturatedVaporPressure` with the plant's saturation curve: $0.611\,e^{17.4T / (T + 239)}\text{ kPa}$.
5. **Dehumidifier RH Setpoint Control:**
   - Upstream `Dehumidifier.java` lacked setpoint control and only triggered above 52% vapor mole fraction ($18\times$ saturation).
   - Added `targetRelativeHumidity` XML attribute (configured to `0.70`). With this, RH holds steadily between $0.700$ and $0.718$.
6. **Shelf Water Reservoir Telemetry:**
   - Exposed shelf reservoir level and capacity (`waterLevel`, `waterCapacity`) over REST from [`Shelf.java`](../src/com/traclabs/biosim/server/simulation/food/Shelf.java) to enable root-zone moisture estimation and truth scoring.

## 3. Subsystems Implemented

### A. Atmosphere & Thermal Dynamics (Phase 1)
- **Martian Sol Diurnal Cycle ([`thermal/model.py`](thermal/model.py)):** Exterior surface temperature $T_{\text{ext}}(t)$ oscillates between $153\text{ K}$ ($-120.15^\circ\text{C}$) and $293\text{ K}$ ($+19.85^\circ\text{C}$) over a $24.65\text{-hour}$ Martian sol.
- **Continuous Thermal Energy Balance ODE:**
  $$C_{\text{th}} \frac{dT_{\text{in}}}{dt} = Q_{\text{lamp}} + Q_{\text{heater}} + Q_{\text{biogenic}} - UA(T_{\text{in}} - T_{\text{ext}}(t))$$
  - Sourced parameters: $C_{\text{th}} = 1,771,225\text{ J/K}$, $UA = 14.0\text{ W/K}$.
  - Analytical exponential relaxation used for unconditional numerical stability at $\Delta t = 1\text{ h}$.
- **Electrical Heating Actuator:** An `Injector` (`Greenhouse_Heater`) draws power from `Power_Store` (up to 5 kW) into `Heater_Sink`, forcing the heater to compete with lamps and life support for battery storage.
- **HVAC Cyber Controller ([`controllers/hvac.py`](controllers/hvac.py)):** Retuned PID controller with hull conduction feedforward ($UA \cdot \Delta T$) regulating cabin temperature to $21.1$–$22.8^\circ\text{C}$ with battery safety reserve protection.
- **ISRU Compressor Model ([`thermal/compressor.py`](thermal/compressor.py)):** Thermodynamic isothermal compression work ($w = \frac{RT}{\eta}\ln\frac{P_{\text{hab}}}{P_{\text{mars}}}$) compressing raw Martian $\text{CO}_2$ ($0.6\text{ kPa}$) to habitat pressure ($70\text{ kPa}$).

### B. Water Recycling & Humidity Loop
- **Closed-Loop Water Flow:** Irrigation draws from `Potable_Water_Store`. `Water_Recycler` (`WaterRS`, Linear) reclaims condensed dirty water from the dehumidifier back into potable water.
- **Root-Zone Reservoir Estimator ([`estimators/water.py`](estimators/water.py)):** Estimates hidden shelf reservoir water from atmospheric vapor balance and crop $\text{CO}_2$ uptake ($0.61\text{ L/mol}$, RMS error $0.33\text{ L}$).
- **Irrigation Controller ([`controllers/irrigation.py`](controllers/irrigation.py)):** Maintains a 24-hour buffer in the root-zone reservoir while regulating dirty water processing through the recycler.

### C. Root-Zone Moisture Instrumentation & Monitoring
- **Root-Zone Level Sensor ([`sensors/root_zone.py`](sensors/root_zone.py)):** Simulates a sump-based reservoir level sensor subject to noise, drift, bias, stuck, and dropout faults.
- **Root-Zone Monitor ([`estimators/root_zone.py`](estimators/root_zone.py)):** CUSUM change-point detector on sensor-vs-estimate residuals, fused inverse-variance level output, and post-harvest re-zeroing to eliminate false alarms after crop replanting.

### D. Atmosphere Instrumentation & Fault Monitoring
- **Atmosphere Sensor Layer ([`sensors/atmosphere.py`](sensors/atmosphere.py)):** Simulates first-order thermal lag ($\tau = 0.5\text{ h}$), linear drift, Gaussian noise, and NDIR optical thermal cross-sensitivity ($\beta \cdot \Delta T$).
- **Temperature & Gas Monitors ([`estimators/thermal.py`](estimators/thermal.py)):**
  - `AtmosphereTemperatureMonitor`: CUSUM detection comparing sensed temperature against thermal model predictions.
  - `SlowChannelMonitor`: Generalized detector for slowly varying channels ($\text{O}_2$ fraction and total cabin pressure) identifying stuck, dropout, and step bias faults without misattributing pressure shifts to the $\text{CO}_2$ sensor.
  - `heater_degraded`: Detects reduced thermal output when heater draws significantly less power than commanded.

## 5. Active Priorities & Next Steps

### Priority 1: Cabin Thermal Survival During Heater Burnout (Fault Tolerance)
- **Problem:** When the heater burns out, the detector catches the failure within 1 tick, but because there is no backup heating, cabin temperature plummets to $-31.5^\circ\text{C}$ on Mars, killing the crop.
- **Solution:** 90% of LED crop lamp power dissipates as heat. When `heater_burnout` or `heater_degraded` is flagged in `obs["actuator_faults"]`, the supervisor must execute an emergency thermal reconfiguration:
  - Force `cmd.crop_power_W` to full power ($2000$–$5000\text{ W}$).
  - This replaces the lost heater energy and keeps cabin temperature safely within the $7$–$32^\circ\text{C}$ survival band.
  - Resolves `@xfail` in `test_cabin_stays_safe_through_heater_burnout`.

### Priority 2: Implement Missing Fault Detectors
1. **$\text{CO}_2$ Injector Stuck Open:**
   - Compare observed $\text{CO}_2$ accumulation rate against commanded injection $\times$ atmospheric mass model.
   - When cabin $\text{CO}_2$ rises without commanded injection, flag `"injector_faults:stuck_open"`.
   - Resolves `@xfail` in `test_co2_injector_stuck_open_is_detected`.
2. **Solar Array Dust Storm:**
   - Compare expected diurnal solar generation curve against actual `power.solar_out`.
   - When actual output falls significantly below expected clear-sky insolation, flag `"solar_faults:dust_storm"`.
   - Resolves `@xfail` in `test_dust_storm_on_solar_array_is_detected`.
3. **Water Recycler Failure:**
   - Compare dirty water store draw against potable water production in `Water_Recycler`.
   - If dirty water accumulates while potable water production ceases under command, flag `"recycler_faults:malfunction"`.
   - Resolves `@xfail` in `test_water_recycler_failure_is_detected`.

### Priority 3: Rebase & Integrate Toni's Water/Power Sensor Layer
- Toni's branch (`toni/irrigation-power-sensors`) implemented noise, bias, and dropout on 8 water and power telemetry keys in [`water_power_sensors.py`](interface/water_power_sensors.py).
- **Task:** Adapt into `sensors/water_power.py` conforming to the `chain_sensor_layers` pattern and modern `TickContext` (`ctx.truth`).

### Priority 4: Supervisory Power Arbitration & Photobiology Integration
- **Photobiology Insights (from `light-subsystem`):**
  - BioSim's lettuce model already counts a 16h photoperiod internally; turning off lamps externally penalizes yield twice.
  - A 72h blackout costs 42% of final harvest at tick 100, but only 5% at tick 550.
- **Task:**
  - Port derived state key `crop.s{i}.ppf_delivered` from `light-subsystem` into [`bindings.py`](interface/bindings.py).
  - Replace the naive collision-checker `merge_commands()` in [`loop.py`](interface/loop.py) with a true Power Supervisor that balances available energy during dust storms:
    - Protect lighting early in crop development (canopy formation).
    - Shed lighting near harvest while prioritizing cabin heating and water recycling.

### Priority 5: Physical ISRU Closed Loop
- Pipe compressed Martian $\text{CO}_2$ moles from `MarsAtmosphereCompressorModel` into BioSim's `CO2_Store` over the simulation loop so the habitat's life support loop is physically closed.

## 6. Architecture & Fault Detection Reference Notes
- **Truth Cleanliness:** `HybridPlant` separates sensor readings from ground truth, and `gh.temp_truth_C` is stripped from `obs` before reaching controllers.
- **Atmosphere Verification:** `scripts/atmosphere_fault_check.py` scores each fault scenario for detection, false alarms, and cabin safety.
- **Gas Model Scaling:** The monitor's $\text{CO}_2$ model uses measured flows (`co2.injected_mol`, `crop.s0.co2_inhaled_mol`) and scales total gas by $P/T$ (preventing the 170 ppm drift over live cycles).
- **Test Requirements:** `tests/test_fault_requirements.py` tracks active and resolved fault detection requirements.
- **Loop Integration:** `estimators.MonitorInLoop` runs the monitor before controllers (as a sensor layer) and steps models after them (as the supervisor). HVAC controls on `fused_temp_C` to survive large sensor biases.
